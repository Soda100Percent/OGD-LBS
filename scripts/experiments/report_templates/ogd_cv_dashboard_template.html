Protocol: Each model's 200-frame data1 sequence is partitioned into 4 folds (for Vroid_*, the first 10% = 20 frames are kept as a core training block; remaining 180 frames form four 45-frame test folds; for Griffin_A2 and Winter_Samurai, 200 frames form four 50-frame test folds). In each fold, Train optimizes skinning weights + bone transforms for 70 iterations; Held-out Test freezes the trained skinning weights (freeze_weights=True) and fits only bone transforms on unseen motion frames. Significance stars (* p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001) compare ssdr12 / ssdr14 against ssdr14v ★ on Held-out Test.
